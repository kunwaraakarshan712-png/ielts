WRITING ASSESSMENT

Understand The Four
Writing Criteria.

Task Achievement / Response

How effectively your response addresses the task and fulfils its requirements.

Coherence & Cohesion

How clearly your ideas are organised and connected.

Lexical Resource

The range, precision and appropriate use of vocabulary.

Grammatical Range & Accuracy

The variety and accuracy of grammatical structures.

Why this matters: IELTS Writing is assessed using four criteria. Understanding these criteria helps you identify what your writing actually needs rather than simply adding complicated vocabulary or grammar.
